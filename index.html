Where I can help

Find the repetitive slice of a job, map the process, then replace the rule-based steps with a script that runs every day. Proven on my own desk first, then handed to the team.

Shrink the idea to the smallest version a stranger can try, then let real usage and feedback decide what comes next. Every iteration gets a reason.

From a store's daily routine to public notes about the work. I watch what breaks in execution and adjust with data and feedback rather than opinion.

Beyond building: pricing, selling, delivering and supporting. Getting the product into a user's hands and keeping it working once it is there.
